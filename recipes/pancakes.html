Pancakes Recipe

Here I am going to describe and explain a few easy steps for a recipe for pancakes.

Ingredients

  • Plain flour: 100g
  • 2 large eggs
  • 300 ml milk
  • 1 tbsp sunflower or vegetable oil
  • lemon wedges
  • caster sugar

Method

  1. Step 1: Put 100g plain flour, 2 large eggs, 300ml milk, 1 tbsp sunflower or vegetable oil and a pinch of salt into a bowl or large jug, then whisk to a smooth batter. This should be similar in consistency to single cream.
  2. step 2: Set aside for 30 minutes to rest if you have time, or start cooking straight away.
  3. Step 3: Set a medium frying pan or pancake pan over a medium heat and carefully wipe it with some oiled kitchen paper.
  4. Step 4: When hot, cook your pancakes for 1 min on each side until golden, using around half a ladleful of batter per pancake. Keep them warm in a low oven as you make the rest.
  5. Step 5: Serve with lemon wedges and caster sugar, or your favourite filling. Once cold, you can layer the pancakes between baking parchment, then wrap in cling film and freeze for up to two months.
